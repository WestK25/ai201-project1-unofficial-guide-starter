CONTENT:
For fall 2026, students should arrive at their assigned move-in appointment. Early arrivals cannot be accommodated. Later arrivals should use their building's move-in hours; after hours, students contact their Resident Assistant for check-in and should expect delays and no normal equipment or volunteer assistance.

Residents personally collect their packet and keys. Up to three guests, including children, may help; guests must leave by 8 p.m. A licensed driver should stay with the vehicle during unloading. After unloading, designated parking is available for at most 45 minutes.

Students present their personalized housing-registration QR code on paper or a phone to staff before check-in. Cleared residents receive keys and check-in materials; helpers receive wristbands. Dates and times depend on the building and last name. Consult the source's fall 2026 schedule for an individual appointment; this summary does not reproduce that schedule.
