CONTENT:
The Bison One Card is Howard's student identification card. Its portal supports virtual ID display, balances, transaction history, deposits of Bison Bucks, and deactivation of lost cards. Students submit a photo through the portal; an initial card requires valid government identification or a passport.

The card supports dining, library services, residence hall identification, shuttle access, and laundry. Laundry points are issued each semester; students use the Speed Queen app and can use Bison Bucks after exhausting points.

Students choose a meal plan in StarRez after a housing assignment. Charges appear on student accounts. Howard describes vegan and vegetarian choices across stations, including dedicated plant-based stations. The page identifies NuVegan and Halal Shack at Blackburn. These are the page's descriptions, not a live menu or guarantee of today's availability.
