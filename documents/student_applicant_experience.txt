Paraphrased historical account by Ornery_Vehicle_8862, who describes reaching the end of freshman year and considering transfer. This is one dissatisfied student's experience, not a representative survey or current policy.

The author reports an initial period without dorm visitors and later visitor restrictions, delays between package delivery and mailroom pickup, limited weekend dining access, and waits or missing items at a food outlet. They also report frustration with late event notices, locked lounges, and difficulty finding community. They acknowledge that other people enjoy Howard and that individual experiences differ.

The author says they enjoy Washington, DC, but are unhappy with aspects of campus social life and some classes. Their statements about sophomore residency and visitation must not replace current official rules; the official housing source describes an approved-exemption process. Historical complaints do not establish today's opening hours or residence policies.
