CONTENT:
Howard's 2026–27 notice lists a $500 housing good-faith deposit upon acceptance into housing and a $50 fall housing application fee. The notice describes these as nonrefundable out-of-pocket payments credited toward charges once actively enrolled.

For fall 2026 and spring 2027, College Hall North and South's one-bedroom/two-bed rate is $7,127 per semester. Drew Hall's one-bedroom/two-bed rate is $4,481 per semester; other Drew room types have different rates. Summer College Hall South standard doubles are listed separately at $1,885 per summer session.

The Crown Jewel meal plan is available to all undergraduates and is listed at $3,578 per semester ($7,157 annually). The Mecca is listed at $3,472 per semester ($6,945 annually). These annual figures are reproduced as published, without recalculating them. This is a selected summary, not the full rate table; room type, eligibility, and billing period matter.
