HOWARD UNIVERSITY TRANSPORTATION SERVICES

Howard University Transportation Services provides free shuttle service for students, faculty, staff, Howard University Hospital affiliates, and visitors conducting business within the Howard community.

SHUTTLE SERVICE

Howard operates fixed shuttle routes with stops near parking lots, residence halls, West Campus, Metro stations, and other University locations.

Most Howard shuttle routes arrive approximately every 20 to 30 minutes. The West Campus route generally arrives approximately every 45 minutes. Actual arrival times may vary because of traffic, weather, road conditions, construction, special events, or other delays.

Students should use Howard's live shuttle tracking service for the most accurate arrival information.

Howard publishes individual schedules and route maps for routes including Metro Express, Clover at The Parks, South Campus, West Campus, 801 N. Capitol Street NW, The Lanes Apartments, Weekend Campus, 640 Massachusetts Avenue, and North Campus.

Shuttle schedules begin on the first official day of classes. The listed times represent shuttle pull-off times.

SHUTTLE GUIDELINES

Riders should carry their Howard University ID and board only at designated shuttle stops. Riders should use headphones when listening to audio. Eating and drinking are not permitted on the shuttle.

Students, faculty, staff, and Howard University Hospital affiliates may use the shuttle. Visitors conducting business within the Howard community may also use the service.

WEEKEND AND HOLIDAY SERVICE

Howard provides a dedicated Weekend Campus Route. Students should check the current published schedule for weekend operating hours and stops.

Shuttle service is suspended during designated University holidays and recess periods. These include Labor Day, Veterans Day, Thanksgiving Recess, Winter Semester Recess, Martin Luther King Jr. Day, Presidents Day, Spring Semester Recess, Memorial Day, and Independence Day.

ADA ACCESSIBLE TRANSPORTATION

Wheelchair-accessible transportation is available upon request from designated shuttle stops. Accessible transportation is typically arranged within approximately 10 minutes.

For ADA transportation assistance, riders can contact the morning service between 7:00 a.m. and 2:00 p.m. or the evening service between 2:00 p.m. and 1:00 a.m.

METRO ACCESS

Several Howard shuttle routes provide access to nearby Metro stations. Students should review the current route maps and schedules to determine which route serves their destination.

Because shuttle arrival times can change, students should rely on the current Howard Transportation Services schedules and live tracking information when planning a trip.
