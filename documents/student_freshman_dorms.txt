Paraphrased individual opinions in a discussion started by an incoming class-of-2030 student. The thread does not establish an objective best residence hall.

Single-Cupcake8787 recommends Drew Hall for the experience. Solo_is_dead distinguishes that experience from amenities. lucioruler25 strongly disagrees with the Drew recommendation and criticizes their experience with Residence Life; the disagreement should not be hidden or turned into a consensus.

lucioruler25 recommends College Hall South (CHS), describing it as coeducational, among the newer dorms, with a bathroom advantage and proximity to a dining hall and other dorms. They suggest Annex or Cook as alternatives. Middle_Main_9145 recommends Cook for its location and bathroom, while Royal-Mine8885 says it is mostly athletes. These are commenters' descriptions, not confirmed eligibility requirements or guaranteed room features.
