Historical student perspectives from the April 2024 discussion, paraphrased; these are not official policy or a current menu.

Commenter theecarameldrop, describing themselves as a finance sophomore, says community can develop through clubs, classes, and residence halls, with students needing to make an effort. They describe career fairs and business internship opportunities, without guaranteeing an internship.

For Blackburn food, theecarameldrop describes build-your-own sandwich, stir-fry, nacho, and pasta stations, plus a vegan station, salad bar, waffles, and a hot-food line that changes daily. They like most build-your-own choices but dislike the pasta, emphasizing personal taste. They prefer Annex food while noting longer lines. They describe Punchout as using dining dollars and warn that buying food elsewhere in DC can be expensive.

MulberryTurbulent685 warns about administrative runaround. Relative_Age3013 advises proactive follow-up and reconfirming information, and suggests walking with others and staying alert at night. These are individual suggestions, not verified safety statistics.
