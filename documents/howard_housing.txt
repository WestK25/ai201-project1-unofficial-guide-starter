HOWARD UNIVERSITY HOUSING APPLICATION

The 2026-2027 Howard University housing application is managed through StarRez.

Before applying, students must pay a nonrefundable $50 housing application fee. Paying the application fee does not guarantee a housing assignment. Students with a prior balance may submit an application, but they will not receive a housing assignment until their student account is clear.

First-time-in-college and second-year students are required to live in University Housing or University-sponsored housing unless they receive an approved housing exemption.

To apply for housing, students log into StarRez using Howard Student SSO, select "Apply for Housing," choose the appropriate academic year, pay the application fee, complete the required information, and submit the application.

Students should monitor StarRez and their Howard email for housing assignment or waitlist updates. If a student receives a housing assignment, they must confirm the assignment in StarRez and pay the $500 nonrefundable housing deposit within three days of receiving the assignment notification.

Housing assignment and waitlist updates are issued on a rolling basis during July. Students may indicate a residence hall preference, but a specific residence hall placement is not guaranteed.

If a student declines an assignment or misses the confirmation deadline, the space may be offered to another student on the waitlist.

Roommate changes may only be requested during fall consolidation week. Hall or room transfer requests are not accepted outside the applicable process.

FIRST-TIME-IN-COLLEGE STUDENTS

First-time-in-college students must complete the required housing eligibility steps. Students confirm their intent to enroll at Howard by paying the required $300 nonrefundable enrollment fee. They must also use their Bison Email for Howard communications and pay the $50 housing application fee through StarRez.

SECOND-YEAR STUDENTS

Second-year students must pay the $50 housing application fee through StarRez. Howard states that second-year students are guaranteed housing.

UPPERCLASSMEN

Upperclassmen may apply for housing by paying the $50 housing application fee through StarRez. However, applying does not guarantee housing for the upcoming academic year.

TRANSFER STUDENTS

Transfer students may also apply by paying the $50 housing application fee through StarRez, but housing is not guaranteed for the upcoming academic year.
